What separates hits from flops (same creator, same niche, same week):
1. **Frame 0 is a full, specific picture.** Hits: a scene, a real clip, a gravestone on a board. Flops: a near-empty dark
   frame. Ep04's grid thumbnail was a blank frame (fixed in code: HOOK_PREROLL).
2. **Hook = a lived moment or a hot take, then "kaise?" by ~5 s.** Never a number or a definition first.
3. **Length is not the enemy.** Hits run 53-164 s. They keep reopening the question: a countdown ("05"),
   "सबसे काम का अभी आ रहा है…", "Shock laga?" at 10 s. Our problem was the first 6 s, not the total.
4. **Characters on screen, big, acting.** billu's flop is the one reel without the duo.
5. **One consistent look per account.** None of them changes style per reel; the hook, topic and format change.
6. **Shares come from things everyone uses** (UPI, Uber, Truecaller, ChatGPT photos) + a specific "us dost ko bhejo
   jo …" line. Hits: shares = 30-90% of likes.
7. **Comments come from an ask you can't miss.** sys.design puts "Comment Codes for PDF" as caption line 1 (14.1K
   comments); devmonk pins "Comment Uber for 1 page PDF"; billu ends on a quiz ("API ya MCP?") and pins the answer.
8. **Original audio, always.** No trending song under the voice-over on any hit.
9. **Bright or busy beats dark and empty.** billu: bright yellow world. devmonk/sys.design are dark but every frame is full.

## The outliers, beat by beat

1. **devmonk 6.6M: "RAC ticket, 2 AM berth"** (system design, Indian Railways)
   - Hook (0-2 s): a felt moment, not a fact. Two passengers share one berth; on-screen "Poora paisa, aadhi berth". Ticket card "RAC 7".
   - Then a time jump "RAAT 2:00 AM" and the question "RAC KYUN?".
   - Body: the system (waiting list → chart batch job → TTE handheld releases no-show berths) told as the night unfolds.
   - Caption: retells the story, then "Follow karo aur comment karo RAC, DM mein bhejta hoon" + "us dost ko bhejo jo regularly train mein jaata hai". Ends with plain search keywords: "rac ticket, rac kya h ...".
2. **devmonk 3.6M: "Toilet se GPS kaise kaam karta hai?"**
   - Hook: an absurd, funny everyday situation (man on a toilet, "Tumhara blue dot") then a zoom out to space.
   - Body: one continuous camera move (toilet → Earth → satellites → quasar) explaining each layer.
   - Caption: one-line question + keywords line "GPS, satellite, quasar, atomic clock, NavIC, ...".
3. **billu 526k: "GitHub pe push ≠ Website LIVE"**

   - Body: "STEP 1, STEP 2…" drawn live on the board with icons and arrows; a checklist with ticks.
   - CTA (other reels): "DM FRONTEND → complete PDF guide", "DM PART 2 → next reel" (a series hook).
   - Comments ask "how do you make these?" and joke about the characters: the duo is the brand.
4. **sys.design.101 2M: "ChatGPT ke 5 codes, face ki lines same"**
   - Hook: a promise + a before/after visual in the first frame, big two-line Hindi headline at the top.
   - Body: a numbered list, one item per beat, each with a demo.

5. **jayantcreates 172k / 141k: "Claude, what did you do?" / "Never ask Claude what it did while you were asleep"**

## Patterns to steal

- **Hook = a moment, a belief, or a line of dialogue.** Never a statistic. The first frame alone must make sense with the sound off.
- **Story world beats slides.** Characters doing something in a place (train, whiteboard, chat with an AI) > cards and charts.
- **Recurring characters are the brand** (billu's duo, jayant's Claude cube). The CHARACTERS repeat; the format, hook and topic never do.
- **Every outlier asks for a comment keyword** ("Comment RAC", "DM FRONTEND", "Comment SYSTEM") with a promised freebie (one-page PDF, prompts, setup). Comments + DMs push reach. Also: "send this to the friend who …".
- **Series**: "Part 2" and "DM PART 2" make people follow.
- **Topics with the biggest pull**: everyday Indian systems (railways, UPI, GPS), practical dev skills (deploy, which framework), AI tricks people can use today, same-day tech news.
- **Captions**: line 1 retells the hook, then the CTA, then a keywords line.
- **Length**: (corrected 26 Nov) the biggest outliers run 53-164 s; what matters is a new picture every 1.5-3 s and an open loop every ~15 s.

## What Debug Bro did wrong (eps 1-3)

- The same skeleton every time (cold open → "15 LPA" chat → … → दोहा). Viewers recognise it after one reel.
- Opening on numbers or facts, dark dashboard look: reads as a lecture.
- No comment-keyword freebie; CTA was a vague question.

## Patterns to steal

- **Hook = a moment, a belief, or a line of dialogue.** Never a statistic. The first frame alone must make sense with the sound off.
- **Story world beats slides.** Characters doing something in a place (train, whiteboard, chat with an AI) > cards and charts.
- **Recurring characters are the brand** (billu's duo, jayant's Claude cube). The CHARACTERS repeat; the format, hook and topic never do.
- **Every outlier asks for a comment keyword** ("Comment RAC", "DM FRONTEND", "Comment SYSTEM") with a promised freebie (one-page PDF, prompts, setup). Comments + DMs push reach. Also: "send this to the friend who …".
- **Series**: "Part 2" and "DM PART 2" make people follow.
- **Topics with the biggest pull**: everyday Indian systems (railways, UPI, GPS), practical dev skills (deploy, which framework), AI tricks people can use today, same-day tech news.
- **Captions**: line 1 retells the hook, then the CTA, then a keywords line.
- **Length**: most outliers are 20-45 s; cuts every 1.5-3 s.

## What Debug Bro did wrong (eps 1-3)

- The same skeleton every time (cold open → "15 LPA" chat → … → दोहा). Viewers recognise it after one reel.
- Opening on numbers or facts, dark dashboard look: reads as a lecture.
- No comment-keyword freebie; CTA was a vague question.
